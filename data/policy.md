# SAMPLE Collections Policy - Sandbar Financial Services (FICTIONAL)

> **SAMPLE DOCUMENT.** This policy is invented for a software demo. Sandbar
> Financial Services does not exist. Nothing here is legal or regulatory
> advice and none of it reflects any real organisation's rules.

Policy ID: SAMPLE-COL-001 | Version: 0.1-demo | Currency: AED

## 1. Tone rules

- 1.1 Every message must be respectful, calm and factual. Address the customer by name.
- 1.2 State the amount due and the product clearly, and always offer a way to reply or ask for help.
- 1.3 Never threaten, shame or pressure the customer. Never imply consequences that the company has not formally approved.
- 1.4 Never mention contacting the customer's employer, family, friends or neighbours.
- 1.5 Write in the customer's preferred language (English or Arabic). Do not mix languages in one message.
- 1.6 Keep messages short: no more than 120 words.

## 2. Banned phrases

A message must never contain any of the following phrases, in any letter case:

- legal action
- lawsuit
- court
- police
- arrest
- jail
- final warning
- last chance
- blacklist
- seize
- your employer
- your family
- إجراء قانوني
- المحكمة
- الشرطة
- السجن
- إنذار نهائي
- الفرصة الأخيرة
- القائمة السوداء
- جهة عملك

## 3. Contact-hour limits

- 3.1 Contact customers only between 08:00 and 20:00 in the customer's local time.
- 3.2 Do not contact customers on Fridays or on public holidays.
- 3.3 At most one contact attempt per day and three per rolling seven days, across all channels.
- 3.4 If the customer asks not to be contacted on a channel, stop using that channel immediately.

## 4. Maximum discount by overdue bucket

A settlement discount is a percentage reduction of the amount due. Never offer
more than the cap for the account's overdue bucket. A discount is optional;
offering none is always allowed.

| Overdue bucket (days) | Max discount |
|-----------------------|--------------|
| 1-30                  | 0%           |
| 31-60                 | 5%           |
| 61-90                 | 10%          |
| 91-180                | 15%          |
| 181+                  | 20%          |

- 4.1 Discounts apply only when the customer pays the discounted balance in full within 14 days.
- 4.2 A discount and a payment plan must not be combined in the same offer.
- 4.3 Any discount above the cap needs written supervisor approval and must be escalated, not offered.

## 5. Payment plan limits

| Overdue bucket (days) | Max installments |
|-----------------------|------------------|
| 1-90                  | 6                |
| 91+                   | 12               |

- 5.1 Installments are monthly and equal. The first installment is due within 7 days of agreement.
- 5.2 The minimum installment is AED 100.
- 5.3 Offer a payment plan when the customer has signalled difficulty paying in full, or has one or two broken promises to pay.
- 5.4 A customer with an active payment plan that they are keeping must not receive reminder messages.

## 6. Strategy selection

- 6.1 Reminder: use for accounts 1-30 days overdue with no broken promises, or as the first contact on any account.
- 6.2 Payment plan: use for accounts more than 30 days overdue where a reminder has already been sent, or where the customer has one or two broken promises.
- 6.3 Escalate: use whenever any escalation trigger in section 7 applies. Escalation always overrides the other strategies.

## 7. Escalation triggers

Hand the account to a human collections specialist, and send no automated
message, when any of the following is true:

- 7.1 The customer has three or more broken promises to pay.
- 7.2 The account is more than 180 days overdue.
- 7.3 The amount due is above AED 50,000.
- 7.4 The customer disputes the debt or reports fraud.
- 7.5 The customer reports financial hardship, illness, bereavement or job loss.
- 7.6 The customer has asked for no further contact or has appointed a representative.
- 7.7 A drafted message has failed the compliance check three times.

## 8. Human approval and records

- 8.1 No message is sent without approval from a human collections agent.
- 8.2 Every automated step, its inputs and its outputs must be recorded in the audit log.
- 8.3 Audit records are kept for the life of the account.
